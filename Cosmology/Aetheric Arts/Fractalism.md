### Introduction
Material reality in Arlos does not resolve into atoms, but into a sixfold elemental paradigm. This fact is undisputed by all but the most eccentric scholars, but to explain how the Six interact in all things, different theories have arisen. The most widespread one in the Golden Age of Aetherics that are the 1420 is **Fractalism**, which holds that all things possess a **Body**, the primary elemental nature that defines their essence. Yet no Body exists alone: each is structured by a **Vein**, the secondary influence that shapes and modifies its expression. Beyond Body and Vein lies the **Humour**, a tertiary coloration that explains the subtle differences even within the same Body–Vein pairing. Together these form the **Body–Vein–Humour triad**, the current limit of human scholarship and instrumentation.

Modern inquiry has revealed that this triadic structure is not accidental but appears **fractal**, giving the theory its name. The sixfold recursion of Fire, Air, Water, Ice, Stone, and Soil appears at every scale where scholars can meaningfully assay materiality. Some researchers therefore suggest that the universe contains **six layers of reality**, each nested within the other, of which Body, Vein, and Humour describe only the first three. If true, three deeper layers remain beyond current reach, hinted at in resonance patterns and harmonic anomalies that no known theory yet explains.
### Bodies, Veins, and Humours
Every material has one **Body**, but its form is bent by a **Vein**. The Body anchors what the thing is; the Vein reveals how it behaves. Six Bodies, each with six Veins, yield thirty-six broad archetypes. And within each Body-Vein pair, six **Humours** are at play, one for each element again. These describe the fine grain of behavior. Thus every Body-Vein archetype subdivides into six Humours, yielding **216 archetypal classes** of matter. This fractal depth explains why two substances may share Body and Vein yet act differently in practice: it is their Humour that marks the difference.
### Beyond Humours
The Fractalist framework holds the triad of Body-Vein-Humour to be the most practical framework of Arlosian natural philosophy. Yet there are enough anomalies or subtle differences even in matter that otherwise aligns in Body, Vein and Humour to suggest further recursion. Some theories exist that suggest three more hidden layers of material reality, completing a sixfold chain, naming them as **Trace,** **Echo, and Crown**, but no consensus exists just yet, even as Scalarism rose to suggests that material reality can be assayed as a composition of all six elements at once. However, despite instruments growing more refined each generation, the deeper mysteries of the world remain elusive, reminding scholars that materiality itself may be far more layered than mortal minds can yet grasp.
