On Arlos, the forces represented by the [[Noble Element|Noble Elements]] are foundational and omnipresent, however mortals choose to name them, fear them, or sanctify them. However, there exist in Arlos beings that are consequential rather than foundational -- of the world as much as in it. These are the Animal Spirits: some nurturing, others terrible, a few fallen into shadow. Revered throughout ancient [[Circle Sea]] cultures -- and even today, particularly outside the dominant [[The Light|Lurist]] faith -- they are distinct from the Noble Elements, but no less ancient, no less mysterious, and no less quasi-divine.

Unlike the Noble Six -- who can choose to manifest where and when they wish -- animal spirits seem more local both in strength and sightings, and their physical forms, when encountered, are usually readable as larger, otherworldly variants of an animal extant in their territory. Whether it is the spirit that defines the animal, or the animal that lends the spirit its shape, has been a matter of debate for as long as mortals have tried to explain the supernatural. The spirits themselves, for all that they generally communicate with mortals much more frequently than the Noble Six, have nothing to say on the matter.

The [[Alvai]], a long-lived, non-human people, are intimately bound to these spirits. Each major Kin of the Alvai carries the physical and spiritual mark of one of the great Spirits -- such as furred limbs, tufted ears, often digitigrade gait -- as a living inheritance from their patron, a bond at once sacred and irreversible. Moreover, it seems reciprocal, as the fate of one side can define the other: the corruption of the [[Bâlghubäerhe|Fallen]] spirits impacted their Kin just as strongly as the diaspora of the [[Moonkin]] impacted the Wolf Mother that is their patron.

## Known Animal Spirits, their bynames, and domains

_The grouping in triads is a purely human affectation, derived from the Lurist habit of dividing things into triplets. In brackets is the elemental coding of the spirit domain, unknown in-world but provided here as a writer's tool._

### Triad of the Circulean
Lumal-Nâri the Wolf Mother, wolf spirit of the icy mountain forests (ISL), diminished in her grief
Ghâl-Vekhu, Brother Bat, bat spirit of the wet jungle canopy (ALW)
Rasha-Tarô the Sun Lion, lion spirit of the horizon-spanning savannah (FAL)

### Triad of the Fallen
Bâl-Torrâl the Iron Bear, bear spirit of the frost-forged highlands (SFI), broken in wrath
Bâl-Urmäat, Grandfather Marrow, bonehook spirit of the bone-bleaching desert sky (AFS), lost to madness
Bâl-Yäeghüul the Night Jaguar, jaguar spirit of the shadowy thickets (LFA), twisted into hatred

### Triad of the Wide-Ranging Ones
Annikhu-Sâti Swift-Fin, seal spirit of the shimmering sea shallows (WSA)
Harangäô Hunger-of-the-Deep, orca spirit of the black-brine trenches (WIF), alone in his hunger
Ankha-Vâl Islands-on-His-Back, turtle spirit of the wandering islands (WLS)

### Triad of the Horned Ones
Rhannukhu Horn-Crowned, urus spirit of the flint-strewn chalk downs (LSF)
Terhakhôl Sure-in-His-Step, goat spirit of the scrubland slopes (ILS)
Talakhâri Runs-like-the-Wind, antelope spirit of the singing brushland (LAF)

### Triad of the Margins
Serhîmi the Seeker, rat spirit of the gutter riches (LSW)
Khima-Kimôtha the Farseer, rook spirit of the rain-battered spires (ASW)
Âsharân the Wayfinder, fox spirit of the open hedges (LAW)

Others
Khonurâti the Patient Coil, Rattlesnake spirit of the heat-shimmering mesas (FSA)
