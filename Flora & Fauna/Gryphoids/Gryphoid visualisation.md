Here are visualisation aides for understanding what the gryphoids of Arlos look like. Since they are all based on the concept of the mystical gryphon -- ie, a merger of an avian front with a mammalian back -- it is easiest to understand gryphoids as such a merger, as outlined below.

Note that generally the avian side helps determine the overall size of the gryphoid, though species can be larger or smaller than either mentioned part.
