## Examples of Remean Names
#### House
Stem of founder's Given Name, a location, etc, + "ion"
#### Matronymic
Given Name of the bearing parent + "nata", with the final "s" of names in 2A assimilating ("-sn-" into "-nn-")
#### Given Name
1A or 2A declension
#### Generational Name
A name referencing an elements in their "nurture" cycle (F>A>W>I>S>L>F), each generation of a family taking a later one (so that one's great-grandparents and great-grandchildren would share the element)
#### Origin Name
Genitive or ê + Ablative of the region or hometown (Eremea is a lexicalized form of the latter)
#### Honorific
Title or rank, with grade or designation

"Innubion Avitinnata Raezavis Nivialvis Valabuiae Magistra Ertanescis"
High Magistra Raezavis, Child of Avitis, Snow-White, House Innubion of Valabuia

"Iovanion Valinnata Cressa Soliastis Eremea Capitolina Remeae"
Capitolina of the Mother City Cressa, Child of Valis, Bitter-Earth, House Iovanion of Remea"

"Calianata Azavis Ignellis ê Ellessertâ Formatris Novioris"
Shaper Junior-Grade Azavis, Child of Calia, Bright-Fire, of Ellesserta
