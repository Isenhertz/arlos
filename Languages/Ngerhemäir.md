Language of the [[Alvai]], of which each kin speaks a different dialect. They have low mutual intelligibility. Often rendered in human languages as "Engelemair".

It is a polysynthetic, head-marking, agglutinative, incorporating, bitonal language, written in an alphabetic syllabary (ie phoneme symbols arranged in discreet units, like Hangul) with diacritics to mark high and low tone. Syllables are structured `(C)V(C)`.

Non-Alvai transcripts are usually unconcerned with bitonal markers no human throat can reproduce, instead using diacritics `äëïöü` to mark a glottal stop following the vowel, and using `âêîôû` instead to mark vowel length doubling instead of the High Peaking tone. Any quote of a Ngerhemäir word in the corpus is by design underdetermined in terms of actual vowel sounds.
