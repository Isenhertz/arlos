### **On the True Nature of Light**

_by Aleth-Vor, itinerant preacher of the Red Hills_  
_Composed circa 460 AR, condemned by the Synod of Three Thrones, 462 AR_

---

> _“He came not with shadow, nor with water, nor with flesh—but with flame. And the fire spoke, and we heard.”_

---

**I. Of the False Division Between Light and Flame**

O you blind! You blind who preach of Light as if it were cold, as if it were a candle held in a hand that does not burn. You scribes in An-Rassor and An-Mirda, you with your inkless words and dry prayers—you speak of the Light as if it were not fire.

But what is light without heat? What is purity without pain?

Did the Prophet not burn at the end? Was he not consumed, wholly, without ash or stink? Is it not told in the **Seventy-Seventh Verse**: _“He rose in fire, and the fire knew him, and he was gone”_?

O fools! The fire did not obey the Light. **The fire _was_ the Light.**

---

**II. Of the Noble One Who Came First**

You name Him Phlogistophor in fear. The Father of Flame. The Noble Heat. The Crown of the Pyre. But I name Him **Lur**.

Not the tame Lur of your sanctuaries, who blesses marriages and oils kings. Not the pale Lur of silk-voiced Hierophants with gold rings and cooled wine. I speak of the **first revelation**, the burning one, the purifier, the **giver of hatred and clarity**, who cast down the false gods of Reme.

Have you forgotten? You _were_ slaves to the shapers. The beast-mongers and mirror-masters who played at becoming as gods. It was not your scribes who broke Reme—it was **flame**. Fire from within. Not siege. Not sword. **Wrath.**

Why do you shudder at this truth?

---

**III. On the Accord Between Church and Element**

Was it not strange—yes?—that the fire came for Reme, just as the Judges declared it a nest of shadow? That the **Book of Judges**, newly inked then, called for its censure, even as its mountains broke and its vaults spilled molten glass?

Do you think the **timing was accident**?

Do you think the Light and Flame war against each other? Nay. They are **one will**.

**Phlogistophor saw the wickedness of the shapers and heeded the cry of the Judges.** Or did the Judges hear _him_? It matters not. What matters is the **concord**. The alignment. The shared blaze.

We, the faithful, burned the heretics. **The Flame burned the city.**

You call it coincidence. I call it **covenant**.

---

**IV. Of Flesh and Salt and Bone**

You say the Elements are not to be trusted. That they are spirits only, lesser than the Light. And yet every rite you keep **requires fire**. You heat your oil. You burn your incense. You cleanse your wounds and your altars. You light your dead.

_Do you think the Light would lend itself to such things if it despised its fire?_

I say to you: the **body is kindling**, the **soul is smoke**, and the **mind is the flickering wick**. Only when all three are offered can the Light be pleased.

---

**V. Of the End and the Great Pyre**

In dream I saw the end. The True Flame returns. Not as whisper. Not as metaphor. Not as a page in the Book of Judges.

He comes as a **towering pyre**, a **breathless wind of cinders**, and all who walk in shadow shall scream and twist and be made clean.

And the faithful—those who welcome flame, who carry the brand, who know the sting—shall be refined, like silver in crucible. Made mirrors. Made light.

You may burn me, but I am already burning. And I shall rise, like the Prophet, through fire.

---

_"If Light is not flame, then why does it hurt the wicked so?"_

---

>Editorial Note by the Synod of Three Thrones
	_This tract was condemned as heretical conflation of elemental animism and sacred doctrine. The author, known as Aleth-Vor the Flamebound, was seized at the gates of Peltham and burned publicly under the supervision of Purifier-Militant Hessadran. All known copies were destroyed save the present fragment, held under seal for purposes of theological warning._
