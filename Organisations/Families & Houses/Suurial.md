> # Suurial (family)
> ![[crest House Suurial.png|Crest of House Suurial]]
> Blazon: _argent a bordure gules, two flag spears gules handled sable barbed or in saltire ; on an enty in point of the second three triangles of the field conjoined_
> Achievement: _A marquis crown_
> Motto: _"We Stand, We Stay"_

House Suurial holds a Marquisate of the March, an old title reaching back to the time that the March [[March of Engalas|Engalas]] was a frontier defending [[Kingdom of Lavion|the Realm]] against southeastern barbarians.
