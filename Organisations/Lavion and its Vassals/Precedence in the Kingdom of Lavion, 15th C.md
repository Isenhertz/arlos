_(Ceremonial Order of Rank and Honor)_

---

## ✦ SOVEREIGNTY

1. **The Queen Regnant / King Regnant of Lavion**  
     _Style:_ _Her/His Sovereign Majesty_  
     _Notes:_ The living fount of law, honor, and divine right. All precedence flows from the Crown.
    
2. **The Crown Consort**  
     _Style:_ _Her/His Royal Highness_  
     _Notes:_ Ranks immediately after the Sovereign, but before all other royals and clergy.
    

---

## ✦ THE ROYAL FAMILY

3. **The Princes and Princesses of the Blood Royal** _(Children and siblings of the Sovereign)_  
     _Style:_ _His/Her Royal Highness_  
     _Notes:_ Direct descendants of the sovereign, not granted lands but enjoy immense privilege.
    
4. **The Princes of the Realm** _(Collateral cadet branches of the Royal House with princely dignity)_  
     _Style:_ _His/Her Serene Highness_  
     _Notes:_ Technically non-heirs but hold ancient princely status and ceremonial rank.
    

---

## ✦ THE ROYAL CROWN VASSAL

5. **The Prince/Princess of Balboa** _(Royal Crown Vassal)_  
     _Style:_ _Her Royal Grace the Principessa Serenissima_  
     _Notes:_ The only territory within Lavion granted this unique, semi-independent status. Ranks immediately after royal kin.
    

---

## ✦ THE CHURCH SUPREME

6. **The Hierophant Primus** _(Pontifex of the Lurist Faith)_  
     _Style:_ _His Most Radiant Sanctity_  
     _Notes:_ Technically above all temporal power in spiritual matters, but ceremonially ranked just below the Crown Vassal in the Lavienese context.
    
7. **The Exarchs of the Sacred Synod**  
     _Style:_ _His/Her Eminence_  
     _Notes:_ Function as royal-level dignitaries in processions and councils. Among them, the **Primicerius of the Book** and **Grand Penitentiary of Light** often take precedence by function.
    

---

## ✦ PEERAGE OF THE HIGH REALM

8. **Archdukes and Archduchesses of the Realm**  
     _Style:_ _His/Her Illustrious Grace_  
     _Notes:_ There are few Archduchies; most have personal union ties to royal blood (e.g. Gaviatolis).
    
9. **Dukes and Duchesses of the Realm**  
     _Style:_ _His/Her Grace_  
     _Notes:_ Ruling high nobles over core territories; ranked by antiquity of title.
    
10. **Marquises and Marchionesses** _(of the Southern Marches and others)_  
     _Style:_ _His/Her Most Noble Lordship/Ladyship_  
     _Notes:_ Border lords with military prestige. Ranked before Counts due to defense obligations.
    
11. **Counts and Countesses of the Realm**  
     _Style:_ _His/Her Right Honorable Lordship/Ladyship_  
     _Notes:_ Typically administrative rather than martial; older titles sometimes outrank younger marquises.
    
12. **Barons and Baronesses**  
     _Style:_ _His/Her Lordship/Ladyship_  
     _Notes:_ Smallest of the landed nobility. Many hold ceremonial seats but little practical power.
    

---

## ✦ ECCLESIASTICAL LORDS AND DIGNITARIES

13. **Patriarchs of the Faith**  
     _Style:_ _His Beatitude the [Title]_  
     _Notes:_ Rank below Archdukes but above Dukes, especially in liturgical or coronation settings.
     
14. **Metropolitan Archbishops**  
     _Style:_ _His/Her Most Reverend Illumination_  
     _Notes:_ Often treated as Counts in precedence, unless personally distinguished.
    
15. **Bishops of the Realm**  
     _Style:_ _His/Her Grace_  
     _Notes:_ Often raised to the peerage via ecclesiastical elevation. Treated as honorary barons in secular settings.
    
16. **Knight-Prelates of the Eternal Dominion**  
     _Style:_ _His Martial Grace_  
     _Notes:_ Treated as Marquises when in military procession, but as Bishops in clerical affairs.
    
17. **Canon-Militant / Abbots and Abbesses**  
     _Style:_ _Reverend Father/Mother_  
     _Notes:_ Defer to nobility in court but hold precedence in religious rites.
    
18. **Lightkeepers and Clerks of the Flame**  
     _Style:_ _Brother/Sister in Light_  
     _Notes:_ No formal precedence, but respected. May dine at noble tables on feast days.
    

---

## ✦ CHIVALRIC ORDERS AND HONORARIES

20. **Knights of the Silent String**  
     _Style:_ _Sir / Dame_  
     _Notes:_ Appointed by the Sovereign. Ranks below barons but above untitled gentry.
    
21. **Royal Advisors, Court Magisters, and Scholars of the Grand Academy**  
     _Style:_ _Magister / Magistrix / Learned One_  
     _Notes:_ Precedence depends on the dignity of office. The Magistra Plenipotentiary of the Academy ranks near Dukes.
    
22. **Common Lords Mayor, Burgomasters, and Gentry**  
     _Style:_ _Lord Mayor / Master_  
     _Notes:_ Often seated after the titled barons but before rural knights and unlanded clergy.
    

---

## ✦ NOTES ON EXCEPTIONS

- **The Grand Academy of the Broken Isles**, while sovereign in theory, defers precedence to Balboa and ranks below Archdukes in all matters but scholarly councils.
    
- **The Sovereign may elevate or demote precedence** in court by **Letters of Honor** or **special decree**, a prerogative used sparingly.
    
- **Foreign Ambassadors** are granted **honorary precedence** just below Dukes unless representing a head of state.
    
- **Heretics and excommunicates** lose all rank at court unless formally restored by the Synod or Crown.
