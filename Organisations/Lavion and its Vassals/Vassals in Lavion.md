## Balboa

Adjective form: Balboan
Principality
Privileged & largely autonomous crown vassal on the northernmost tip of Ertra; almost synonymous with progress and refinement
Borders Gaviatolis (S)
Counties: City of Balboa, Grand Academy of the Broken Isles, Barony of Caldasci, City of Rivazziano, Barony of Maristagna

## Gaviatolis

Adjective form: Gaviatolian
Archduchy
Ancient rival of Balboa on the Ertrese Peninsula, now in personal union through marriage
Borders Balboa (N), Nikapolante (SW), Fiurisci (S)
Counties: Barony of Arolessa, City of Vessantera, Barony of Seravente, Barony of Pietravola, Barony of Terisante, Barony of Giustavera

## Nikapolante

Adjective form: Nikapolantean
Archduchy
Foot of the Ertrese Peninsula, rich agriculture
Borders Gaviatolis (NE), Fiurisci (E), Cendrelac (SW)
Counties: City of Portoclaro, Barony of Vanikante, Barony of Valanession, Barony of Seranthos, City of Serranika, Barony of Ventrokaston

## Devrascoigne

Adjective form: Devrascan
Duchy
Coastal lands north of Virell
Bordering Corvandie (E), Lavion (SE), Parthall (S), Nicavre (W), Rasselune (NW)
Counties: Barony of Montescaut, Barony of Cendreval, Barony of Bellemar, Barony of Cuironne, Barony of Estremont, Free City of Port-Arquillon, Free City of Saint-Marelle, Free City of Val-du-Sel

## Nicavre

Adjective form: Nicavrenne
Duchy
Westernmost duchy, hugging the slopes of the Wolves' Teeth with passes to the Commonwealth and Svenu
Bordering Rasselune (N), Devrascoigne (E), Parthall (SE), Vauntryre (S)
Counties: Earldom of Montdore, Earldom of Loupierre, Earldom of Rochevaux

## Lavion

Adjective form: Lavienese (also for the Barony and Kingdom)
Duchy
Held by the Crown
Heartland of the Kingdom; sits astride the Mevrille, a tributary to the Upper Varsa, where the ancestral castle of the royal family guarded an important ford
Bordering Devrascoigne (N), Corvandie (NE), Mirevigne (S), Parthall (SW)
Counties: City of Virell, Sanctuary of Aramoor, Barony of Lavion, Barony of Cresselin, Barony of Verduc, Barony of Montivrel

## Parthall

Adjective form: Parthallan
Archduchy
Held by a junior branch of House de Lavion
Central Lavion, astride the Upper Varsa; confluences with three tributaries (the Mirevant, the Forialle, the Tiévre) make this some of the most fertile lands of the realm
Borders Devrascoigne (N), Lavion (NE), Mirevigne (SE), Cyntere (S), Vauntryre (SW), Nicavre (NW)
Counties: Barony of Haut-Part, Barony of Tressac, Barony of Boisverne, Barony of Valcreux, Barony of Cambralet, Free City of Pont-Part, Free City of Chavrelle

## Corvandie

Adjective form: Corvan
Duchy
Held by the Crown
Coastward of Virell; controls the Lower Varsa and its estuary as well as the coast west of the estuary
Bordering Cendrelac (E), Mirevigne (S), Lavion (SW), Devrascoigne (W)
Counties: Barony of Argennae, Barony of Varcel, Barony of Sudmere, Free City of Pont-Varse

## Cendrelac

Adjective form: Cendrelese
Duchy
Easternmost Lavienese duchy in the plains before the foothills of the Ertrese Peninsula 
Borders Nikapolante (NE), Fiurisci (E), Engalas (S), Mirevigne (W), Corvandie (NW)
Counties: Barony of Cendreux, Barony of Lacenne, Barony of Montcendre, Barony of Gravet, Barony of Barbarigo

## Rasselune

Adjective form: Rasselan
Duchy
Northwestern tip of the realm, neighbouring the Commonwealth; considered a bit of a backwater despite a rich maritime tradition and deliberative practices in its guilds that show the influence of the Commonwealth
Bordering Devrascoigne (SE), Nicavre (SW)
Counties: Free City of Paltos, Barony of Varnecourt, Barony of Saint-Paltren, Barony of Lunecarre, Barony of Rasnac

## Mirevigne

Adjective form: Mirevien
Duchy
Largest duchy of the realm south of the Varsa; takes its name from the Mirevant, a tributary of the Varsa, that forms its western border with Parthall 
Neighbouring Lavion (N), Corvandie (NE), Cendrelac (E), Engalas (SE), Treglavien (S), Cyntere (SW), Parthall (W)
Counties: Barony of Mirefoux, Barony of Vignedore, Barony of Pressan, Barony of Sautret, Barony of Clos-Ambray, Barony of Tannereve, Barony of Marignac, Free City of Varenne, Free City of Pont-Mire

## The Marches

### Engalas

Adjective form: Engalian
March (Privileged Barony)
Tasked with guarding the southern Lupine pass through which horsemen raiding parties used to flow into the realm; now profits from trade with Karsova but still wary of raiders
Bordering Cendrelac (NE), Mirevigne (NW)

### Fiurisci

Adjective form: Fiuriscan
March (Privileged Barony)
Easternmost march at the foothills of the Ertrese Peninsula; guards the pass to Lu-An's western reach, famous for being the staging grounds for a failed crusade against the Sunkin
Bordering Nikapolante (NW), Gaviatolis (N)
In personal union with Balboa

### Vauntryre

Adjective form: Vauntrian
March (Privileged Barony)
Westernmost March in the Wolves' Teeth; set to guard against mountain clans and Svenish raiders
Guards the pass to Svenu
Bordering Nicavre (N), Parthall (E), Cyntere (SE)

### Cyntere

Adjective form: Cynteral
March (Privileged Barony)
Guards the pass to Varngard
Bordering Vauntryre (NW), Parthall (N), Mirevigne (NE), Treglavien (E)

### Treglavien

Adjective form: Treglavienese
March (Privileged Barony)
Guards the pass at the headwater of the Mirevant that allows travel to both Varngard and Karsova
Bordering Mirevigne (N), Cyntere (W)
