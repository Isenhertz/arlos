_“Not of Crown nor Foculier, but of Oath and Sword.”_  

The Eternal Dominion is a loose confederation of zealous [[The Light|Lurist]] crusaders, whose knight-monasteries dot the [[Circle Sea]]. They are politically influential due to their allegiance to the [[Hierophant]], but mostly their eyes are turned outward in their quest to spread the Light of Lur to heathen lands.

The Eternal Dominion has existed, in one form or another, for as long as Lurism, and was particularly strong in the ancient days, when its crusaders subjugated and then eradicated the ancient empire of [[Reme]]. It predates the [[Theocracy of An-Suur]] by several decades, and can be considered its non-governmental, military arm.

While diminished from its golden age, the Dominion remains a stubborn presence, especially in remote or unstable borderlands.

---

### **1. Structure & Ideology**

- **The Grand Convocation**:  
    A rare gathering of the Preceptors (leaders) of major Orders, used to resolve disputes or elect a **High Marshal**, who acts as the ceremonial and sometimes functional head of the Dominion.
    
- **Orders of Note**:
    
    - _Order of the Revelation_: once the most prestigious; now scattered across ruins, their remaining brothers act as relic-keepers and spiritual duelists.
        
    - _Order of the Roaring Flame_: fanatical purifiers, often accused of brutality. Still active along the southern frontiers.
        
    - _Order of Saint Adamar_: famed for discipline and deep theological learning; maintains strong ties with An-Suur's Patriarchate.
        
- **Founding Ethos**:  
    The Dominion believes it serves **the True Will of the Divine** directly -- an unmediated interpretation of sacred duty. It reveres the Hierophancy, but claims a **parallel authority** born of oaths and sacrament, not hierarchy.
    

---

### **2. Relationship to the Hierophancy**

_“Brothers in doctrine; rivals in sovereignty.”_

#### Cooperation:

- The Orders often protect pilgrims, shrines, and remote parishes where Hierophantic clergy are sparse.
    
- The Ordinate of the Flame and the Dominion sometimes **cooperate in rooting out heresy**, particularly in fringe provinces or during theological purges.
    
- Some Patriarchates (especially An-Suur and the Drowned Coast) view the Orders as **guardians of spiritual purity**, essential to keeping the faith from dissolving into compromise.
    

#### Conflict:

- The Dominion **rejects centralized ecclesiastical control**. They often refuse direct orders from the Hierophant Primus or even local Patriarchs unless those orders align with “Sacred Oath.”
    
- The **Second Hierophancy** tried to incorporate the Orders into its military apparatus. This failed, leading to centuries of **mutual suspicion**.
    
- The Dominion's autonomous theology occasionally borders on **heresy**, especially its belief that divine authority flows through holy action, not clerical consecration.
    

---

### **3. Relationship to Secular Power**

_“They stand beside the crown, never beneath it.”_

#### With Lavion:

- Lavion **tolerates** the Orders, especially in border zones where royal influence wanes.
    
- During the **Lavionese Civil War**, some Orders fought **against the crown**, siding with the King in the South due to Lavion's encroaching secularism.
    
- In the reign of **Queen Lurie-Mavrel**, the Dominion is viewed with **quiet suspicion**, though she allows them to operate so long as they do not claim territorial sovereignty.
    

#### With Balboa:

- Balboa maintains **an armed neutrality** with the Dominion. Its old port is home to a small **Preceptory of the Order of the Gilded Anchor**, which guards relics recovered from Circle Sea expeditions.
    
- Lauralotta di Balboa is not hostile, but sees them as **anachronistic and ideologically dangerous**. She keeps a close eye on their presence in the upper passes of the Wolves’ Teeth.
    

#### With Border Territories:

- In territories with **weak governance** (mountain passes, river deltas, desert fringes), the Orders sometimes **function as de facto rulers**.
    
- Peasants and local lords alike may look to the Dominion for **justice, defense, and theological arbitration**.
    

---

### **4. Current Status**

- **Power Base**: Fragmented but persistent. They control **fortified monasteries** in strategic locations (mountain passes, old trade roads, relic sites).
    
- **Numbers**: Greatly reduced from their height -- likely a few thousand knight-brothers, and twice that number of lay warriors and initiates.
    
- **Political Role**: No longer kingmakers, but still influential in **regional affairs**, especially during succession crises or external threats.
    

---

### **5. Cultural Influence**

- The Orders **preserve martial liturgies**, sacred chants, and relic-trials no longer practiced by the main Hierophantic church.
    
- They are **both feared and romanticized** in popular imagination -- symbols of unbending faith and a purer, nobler past.
