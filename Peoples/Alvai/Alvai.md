The Alvai are a long-lived, non-human people found across the Circle Sea basin and beyond, each of their major Kin intimately bound to one of the great Animal Spirits. The bond is at once sacred and irreversible: a Kin's animalistic features -- furred limbs, tufted ears, slit or dish-black eyes, claws proper to their patron -- are not mere biology, but a living inheritance from the Spirit that claimed them.

In general terms the Alvai run tall and lithe, though the spectrum across Kin is wide: a Starkin adult might reasonably be measured at a foot and a half-pole tall and be deemed average, whereas the tallest of Sunkin warriors who ever lived, Majira the Sundered, reportedly stood a full pole tall at rest, towering over all her contemporaries like a solitary mountain over the lowlands.

Common to all the Kin (as they call themselves) are animalistic features, differing by patron, but all are furred from the waist down. Body hair generally tends to come in tufts along spine or forearms -- pale, blond, and silvery tones in Moonkin, coppery gold and reds in Sunkin, dark blues and blacks as well as greys in Starkin -- and their hair can in some become a mane marrying the shoulders to the head; facial hair, too, is common. Claws and feet differ by patron: the Starkin bear sharp digitigrade talons with a raised heelspike, the Sunkin, also digitigrade, have padded feline hindpaws, and the Kin of the Moon instead have fully planted, though furred and visibly clawed, feet.

A trait shared by all Kin is the nature of their voice, described as both sonorous and ethereal -- a fact owed to their naturally occurring bitonality, akin to what in humankind is approximated in techniques like throat singing. Alvai speak with two independent tones at once, which features heavily in the dialects of their native language, [[Ngerhemäir]], whose syllables carry a high and a low tone that differentiate a word's meaning. In human tongues that lack such tones, the effect can alternately be enchanting or haunting, depending entirely on which tone the Alvai chooses to give their words.
