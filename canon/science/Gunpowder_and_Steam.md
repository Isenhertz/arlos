Gunpowder and steam should both reasonably be drivers of Arlosian industrialisation, as they were on Earth. So, why aren't they?

Because on Arlos, sprite can and will occur or be drawn to scenes and objects of significant elemental "charge" (for want of a better word). Gunpowder and indeed any kind of explosive mixture is of such interest to sprites of Fire that they are functionally unusable other than as bait for these elementals ("gunpowder" therefore does not even exist as a concept or word -- everyone knows that guns use air or, rarely, aetheric modulation). Steam, likewise, draws sprites of Water and Air that are nice enough to look at when they play in the vapor rising from a kettle, but present serious problems when they interfere with a boiler. A cultural and religious mistrust of steam further relegates it to niche and household use where the scale is not large enough to incite sprites to cause mischief.
