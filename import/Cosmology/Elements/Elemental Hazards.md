In Arlos, all change arises where the six elements meet by relation, and it is the Weak relations -- the abeyant and the antagonistic -- that most readily answer aetheric touch. The Strong chain of adjacencies provides the world’s dependable background, but the Weak are the world's habits in flux, the seams where structure, resonance, and materiality can slip in to effect change. But when they are jostled past their manners, they can turn into calamities rather than miracles.
## Cohesive Shear

Cohesive Shear is Water-Stone gone slack. Mortar powders under a brush; leather parts along the grain; barrel staves refuse to swell tight; tool edges will not take or hold a hone. In bodies the telltales are bleeding gums, loosening teeth, hair that thins and comes away in clumps, and in the long run the dull ache of organs failing their own seams. Streets show it as fine dustings along brick courses; carved details shed crisp grit; packed earth paths unravel into flour.

## Fulminant Discharge

Fulminant Discharge is the Air-Ice relation loosed as sudden pathfinding spark. Charge hairs along rails and pump rods, then leaps with a dry snap; blue whiskers halo metal corners; thin black fern marks etch themselves into planks. People taste tin and sour wind, feel pinprick lashes across the skin, fingers jerk, hearts stumble, and nerves misfire in stuttering cascades. In still rooms a prickle rises before the strike; in open yards the air thins and sharpens, then answers with a single decisive crack.

## Motile Drift

Motile Drift is the Air-Stone relation turned errant. Benches creep across decks; plumb bobs refuse to hang true; hanging lamps swing in windless air; earthworks settle a finger width and stop with perverse neatness. Walkways feel faintly tidal underfoot; stacked goods slowly skew themselves out of square. People report vertigo in quiet rooms, a sense that the floor breathes, and dreams of sliding. In the grand extreme, whole landscapes take to travel -- most famously the Sifted Fields of [[Lu-Pto]], where silt and sand act as if the very ground had forgotten to settle down.

## Volatile Bloom

Volatile Bloom is the relation between Fire and Water acting up in a temper. Oils flash to vapor in cold air; kettles seem to boil from within; cisterns stratify into hot and cool layers that overturn without warning; still puddles ghost-steam with no flame to show for it. Flesh runs fevers that spike and break, sweat vanishing so fast the skin chills, then returning in sudden flushes. Workshops hiss and hush without pattern; spirit lamps flare, gutter, and flare again without a sizzlewick to account for it; storerooms smell alternately of hot metal and wet stone.

## Tensive Rive

Tensive Rive is the clean, cruel face of Fire-Stone under strain. Rods and rails sing on the edge of hearing; hairline cracks open with glittering tidy edges; nails spit; dry masonry crazes in fans of mica sheen. Teeth ache as if struck; fingers twitch when they touch a handrail; a brief, toothy brightness darts along metal and is gone. Bridges answer with a glassy ring; kiln tiles star with crisp lines as though scored by a knife of light.

## Reactive Dearth

Reactive Dearth is Reactivity suppressed -- Ice’s hush over Soil’s go. Fields green but do not grow; worts sit sweet and quiet; compost turns cool and odorless; tannery vats refuse to take. Flesh mirrors it with numb-and-blue fingers, blood that clots too eagerly in cold limbs, bowels that stall, and skin that bruises like glass from small knocks. City air tastes stale; smoke hangs pale and suffocating; damp cellars feel dead rather than dank.

## Gravitic Weigh

Gravitic Weigh is Attraction grown burdensome and lawlike. Fog drops like felt and lies heavy; dust falls out of the air too completely and rooms go over-quiet; banks slip without rain; vaults feel low, as if the ceiling had learned to lean. Airships trim in an unseen mire, rising slow and settling quick; ballast creeps downward within its own bins. People complain of throbbing temples, calves that tire early, and ears that pop in weather that seems otherwise still.

## Saturation Slough

Saturation Slough is Water and Soil arguing until the ground toggles between sponge and soup. Street joints weep; cellar steps sweat; a firm bank turns to pudding under a single cart and then, perversely, stiffens like a fist an hour later. Bodies echo with swollen ankles, wet lungs, and bowels that either sluice or seize. Timber swells past fit and then shrinks with sudden gaps; plaster blooms damp maps that fade and return in the same places.

## Thermic Lash

Thermic Lash is Fire-Ice Antagonism snapping like a whip. Glass sings and goes to crumbs; hot rivets seize in cold flanges; clay tiles craze in tight spidering; iron sweats and then blooms red within a watch. People shiver in rooms that read warm, then break into sweats in a slight draught; frostnip and flash burn trace the same accident line. Streets crack in neat arcs after a bright noon and a clear night; stonework pops thin plates as if shedding scales.
