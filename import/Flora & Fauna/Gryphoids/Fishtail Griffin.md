The Fishtail or Maritime Griffin is a coastal apex predator, characterized by its avian head, wings, talons and feathers, and a strong tail ending in a flipper.  
 
Several subspecies of maritime griffin exist, filling a variety of niches, from the Whitebeak Fishtail that feeds exclusively on river fish to the Giant Fishtail that is large enough to hunt seals and many smaller cetaceans.
