The Princely House of Balboa has ruled over the [[Principality of Balboa]] since long before the [[War of the Three Kings]] made them a privileged vassal of the crown. Their colors are blue and silver, their sigil a long-handled axe, referencing the mythical axe Treebiter. Their motto is "Cutting Both Ways".

## Genealogy and Notable Members

### Age of Enlightenment

- **Giulianiste Balbo** _(c. 120 AR)_  
    A famed merchant-admiral of semi-legitimate origin, Giulianiste leveraged Balboa's strategic location and naval strength to seize control amid the power vacuum left by the Fall of Reme. Declared _Magister Portum_ by the ruling patrician council in recognition of his de facto command, he ruled as a military strongman with a commercial mandate.

- **Cassan Balbo / Cassan di Balboa** _(c. 160 AR)_  
    Grandson of Giulianiste. Consolidated the city's political structure, transforming Giulianiste's maritime autocracy into a formalized hereditary principality. Adopted the style _di Balboa_, founded the ruling House, and became the first to hold the title _Principe of Balboa_. Oversaw the early construction of the [[Princely Palace]].

### Dark Ages

Balboa embraced Lurism early, under strong Eternal Dominion influence, and its influence over Ertra grows by military might as much as mercantile means. The "Five Generations of Expansion" in the Dark Ages come to a close with devastating losses against Gaviatolis, local rival state on the peninsula, and the subsequent turn inwards. The Balboan military, once a force of conquest, is restructured into free mercenary companies. This would define the city's economic and military strategy for centuries.

### Age of Corsairs

During this era, Balboa's fleets were notorious across the Circle Sea. Though technically "privateers," many captains engaged in open piracy -- licensed unofficially by successive Principes to fund the city's growing wealth.

Notable rulers include:

- **Romina Serillana di Balboa** _(r. 978-1002 AR)_  
    A widowed Prinicipessa who ruled with an iron grip during the Corsair Boom. Commissioned the Fleet Charter, ensuring revenues from plunder enriched the state coffers.    

### Consolidation and Privileged Autonomy

- **Giancarle di Balboa** _(1088-1159 AR; r. 1109-1158 AR)_  
    A master statesman and soldier. During the War of the Three Kings (1117-1121), he played all sides diplomatically while dispatching mercenaries to support different contenders. Balboa was eventually besieged, but Giancarle negotiated a surrender under extraordinarily favorable terms.  
    The Principality became a direct vassal of the Lavienese crown, retaining nearly all its privileges, including internal autonomy and control over its military companies. The House di Balboa became "crowned without a crown."    

### Modern Period

- **Principe Vittorio II. di Balboa** _(1357-1401, r. 1380-1401 AR)_  
    A conservative but competent ruler. Vittorio invested heavily in the Academy of the Broken Isles and expanded Balboa's influence through learned patronage rather than arms. A voice of moderation in the Lavienese succession crisis of 1401, he died under suspicious circumstances.
    
- **Carlorus Giulianno Erminguardo Victorior di Balboa** _(1382-1404, r. 1401-1404)_
	Acceded in the middle of the succession crisis of the realm. Died unexpectedly in 1404 from an infection following a duel, after which his younger sister inherited the throne.
    
- **Lauralotta Victualia Emmanuela Augusta Terisea di Balboa** _(b. 1389 AR, r. 1405–)_  
    The current _Principessa Serenissima_. Known for her monumental stature (both physical and political), fashion dominance, and aromantic aloofness. A patron of learning and progressive reform -- though her power is still tightly wound into the ancient oathbound structure of Balboa's mercenary and patrician elite.
