> # Balboa (Principality)
> ![[Balboa (principality).png]]
>Blazon:  *argent a long axe sable impaled with azure a fishtail griffin argent*
>Achievements: *a royal helmet sable with a crest of a Naval Crown Proper, shewn with a mantling of a cloak pavilion proper*
>Supporters: *to the dexter a fishtail griffin sable armed and beaked or, to the sinister a mermaid argent*
>Motto: *"At Her Mercy We Prosper"
>Capital: [[City of Balboa]]

The Principality of Balboa is a small feudal state situated on the rugged coast of the northern tip of the Ertran Peninsula. Centered around the [[City of Balboa]] that gave the city-state its name, it was founded as a [[Remean Empire|Remean]] port in -687 AR, and weathered the fall of the Empire to become a local power in its own right. As the [[Kingdom of Lavion]] rose in the west, the two states came into conflict, culminating in the [[War of the Three Kings]] of 1117-1121 AR, which ended with the surrender of Balboa and its integration into the Kingdom of Lavion as a privileged Crown Vassal.

Though the Principality of Balboa has been a peaceful vassal of the realm for over three centuries, it retains much of its ancient and singular character. As a city-state home to prosperous traders and renowned mercenary companies, it stands out like a jewel among the more agrarian regions surrounding it, and even gets compared favourably with the crownlands of the [[Kingdom of Lavion|Lavienese]] capital, [[Virell]].

The Principality hasn't gone to war in the centuries since formally joining the realm as a vassal state, though its mercenary companies have seen their fair share of conflicts in the intervening years -- sometimes on opposing sides. However, it still retains a strong military culture in its traditions and ceremonies, such as the troop review held every five years. A parade is performed by the different companies marching through the streets of the city and past the balcony of the Princely Palace, and in the closing ceremony they renew their oaths not to take up arms against their home city.

### Early Period - Remean Rule and the Fall

**-687 AR:** Founding of Balboa as _Portus Valabonicus_, a maritime trade port and naval station of the Remean Empire. Legend has it that it was named for _Admiral Valabona_, who established the permanent harbor at the mouth of the River Ertra. -650 AR is also mentioned, but seems to refer to the hilltop fort protecting the port rather than the settlement itself.

**-680 to 0 AR:** Balboa thrives as a semi-autonomous provincial capital and crossroads for [[Circle Sea]] trade. Governed by appointed Procurators from [[Reme]]. Flourishes culturally and economically.

**0 AR - The Fall of Reme:** The eruption triggered by the failed summoning of [[Phlogistophor]] buries the imperial capital. With the [[Eternal Dominion]] pushing in from the north and chaos spreading, Reme's coastal colonies are left leaderless. With the empire in ruins, Balboa survives through sheer luck as the upheaval of the conflagration forces the Dominion's warships to make landfall further south, sparing the city from conquest and ransacking. Local families -- not yet nobles -- scramble for power in the ensuing vacuum.

### The Rise of House Balboa (Age of Enlightenment)

**c. 35 AR:** A council of local patricians is ruling Balboa in absence of Imperial authority. Trade continues on the Gyres, and Remean traditions slowly evolve into local variants.

**ca. 55 AR - Lurist Pressure from the North:** Missionaries and warbands of the Eternal Dominion are ever pressing southward in the crusades against the Moonkin of the southern Wolves' Teeth Mountains. Balboa, deeply pragmatic and mercantile, readily welcomes the worship of Lur in its walls to maintain peace with emerging northern powers. The faith spreads gradually, even given the established reverence of [[Maregina]]. Balboa avoids direct confrontation, deftly navigating between zealots and heretics.

**c. 120 AR:** A prominent merchant-admiral, Giulianiste Balbo, uses his fleet to seize control of the harbor. He consolidates power through alliances, and the council names him Magister Portum.

**c. 160 AR:** Giulianiste’s grandson, Cassan Balbo, adopts the hereditary title of _Principe_ (Prince) and refashions his family name as [[di Balboa]].

**c. 250 AR - First Princely Compact:** House di Balboa is firmly established, ruling Balboa and its hinterlands as an independent maritime principality.

###  Ambition, War, and Expansion in the Dark Ages

**410-710 AR - The Five Generations of Expansion:** Balboa builds a powerful fleet, expanding its influence to surrounding coastal towns. Princely power grows unchecked, with periods of great prosperity and notorious decadence.

**824 AR:** A disastrous war with nearby Gaviatolis weakens Balboa's standing. The principality retreats from its territorial holdings in the south of the peninsula, but its maritime power remains.

**ca. 830 AR - Formal Establishment of the Balboan Condottieri:** In response to the instability following from the military defeat and the political turn inwards, the Balboan regiments (themselves patterned after the original Remean legions stationed in the port) become free mercenary companies. These are granted great autonomy, and soon gain fame for discipline, flair, and brutality in battle.

**ca 900-1300 AR - The [[Age of Corsairs]]:** Turned away from open warfare, Balboa wages private wars with various pirate princes and rogue states in the Circle Sea, blurring the line between commerce and piracy. The city becomes legendary for its maritime prowess, its dual-purpose merchant-warships, and the flamboyance of its captains.

**c. 1000 AR:** Tensions grow between the principality's governance and the claim of Genovrast to sovereignty over the Ertrese Peninsula.

### The War of the Three Kings and Royal Vassalage (1117-1121 AR)

**1117 AR:** The War of the Three Kings breaks out after the Lavienese royal succession crisis following the death of King Oswine de Lavion is settled when Roburic, Oswine's uncle, takes the crown of Lavion. Cavrian de Nevron, ruler of the lands along the northern slopes of the Wolves' Teeth Mountains that will be Mirevigne, seizes on a perceived moment of weakness and presses his claim as "King in the South", throwing the realm into civil war. Balboa, initially allied to the King in the South, finds itself at odds with Lavion, and its fragmented condottieri end up fighting on all sides.

**1119 AR - The Siege of Balboa:** Despite fierce resistance, the city’s defenses are worn down over the course of a siege lasting over two years. Blockade runners and smugglers keep the populace fed, but attrition takes its toll on the armed forces.

**1121 AR:** Balboa surrenders. However, thanks to shrewd diplomacy by Principe Giancarle di Balboa, the terms are unusually generous:
- House Balboa retains control of the city and title.
- The Principality becomes a privileged crown vassal of the King of Lavion, owing fealty directly rather than be subsumed under local dukes.
- Balboa retains the right to mint coin, regulate commerce, and appoint officials.
- Balboa is exempted from most royal levies, in exchange for supporting the crown with elite mercenaries and naval force projection.
This marks the beginning of the Second Princely Compact.

**1122 AR - Establishment of the Azure Codex:** Named for the heraldic colors of Balboa (azure and silver), the Codex regulates the rights and duties of the mercenary companies under the new framework of Balboan autonomy under Lavienese sovereignty. From this point forward, the companies swear oaths never to take contracts against Balboa, and all new captains must be licensed by the reigning Principe or Principessa.

### Postwar Period and the Modern Line (1121-1405 AR)

**1150-1250 AR:** The di Balboa dynasty reinvents itself as patrons of science, diplomacy, and fashion. Balboa’s influence in courtly and scholarly circles steadily increases.

**1274 AR:** Balboa becomes the primary Lavienese port for Circle Sea diplomacy. The Balboan cadet branch House Kobinhaven is founded in the Commonwealth principality of the same name.

**1389 AR - Birth of Lauralotta Victualia Emmanuela Augusta Terisea di Balboa:** The second child of Principe Vittorio II., she is never expected to rule. Her intellect is quickly discovered, and she is raised under the best tutelage imaginable, including studying at the Grand Academy of the Broken Isles.

**1405 AR:** At age 16, Lauralotta Victualia Emmanuela Augusta Terisea di Balboa accedes to the title of Principessa Serenissima after her older brother Carlorus Giulianno Erminguardo Victorior di Balboa dies of an infection after a duel. Her reign is marked by eccentric brilliance, lavish patronage, and her famed interventions in fashion, diplomacy, and scholarship.
