## The Sacred Orders

The Sacred Orders serve as the functional arms of the faith. The Ordinate of Scribes curates the sacred texts, keeps doctrinal histories, and advances aetheric theological commentary; its academies staff embassies and train synodal clerks, and many Harmonists trace their formation to its schools. 

The Ordinate of Mercy cares for the poor, sick, and orphaned and is often the most visible face of the Church in cities and towns, having absorbed and re‑christened many pre‑Lurist caregiving customs. 

The Ordinate of the Flame preserves orthodoxy and answers heresy through carefully delimited Writs of Censure that require the joint consent of a regional Patriarch and the Hierophant or an Exarchal proxy -- a constraint learned from the excesses of the Second Hierophancy. The Flame is the natural home of Antidiscordianist rigor and the ecclesiastic ally of the Eternal Dominion when the Church Militant is summoned, but it is by no means a monolithic bloc of conservative thought. 

Alongside these ordained stand lesser orders, lay brotherhoods and sisterhoods, devotional guilds, monastic agents, itinerant preachers, and peacekeepers. Together they form a tapestry of people bound together in service to the Light.
