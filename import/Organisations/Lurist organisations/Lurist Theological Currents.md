
These are some of the minor schools of theological thought attached to the principal currents of Lurist doctrine. All are in good standing and remain under the patronage of their parent traditions; none are large enough to shift ecclesiastical policy on their own.

## Orthodoxy

_The traditional via media -- scriptural fidelity and liturgical consensus._

1. **Ember Psalters (Psalterians)**
	The Psalterians preserve early chant lines reputed to descend from the Prophet. Their rural scholae pass melodies by ear rather than by notation, trusting that the right tone steadies the soul. Most active in provincial seminaries and cathedral towns, they hold that melody can carry plain meaning without adornment.

2. **Guardians of Verse 777 (Ultimoversians)**
	Taking their name from the final verse of the Book of Verses, the Ultimoversians model interpretive humility and communal clarity. They preside over mediation tables, stage disputations, and read the Lore of Light with spare gloss for the laiety. Active in diocesan courts and mixed-rite borderlands, they are the first choice to temper excess on either side.

3. **Bearers of the Creed (Libraferentians)**
	The Libraferentians conserve and recite canonical codices with exacting care. Their copy rubrics, collation of variant witnesses, and reading vigils make them the conscience of scriptoria tied to patriarchal archives. Suspicious of hasty allegory, they serve as steady doctrinal bellwethers.

## Antidiscordianism

_A theology of purification, militant clarity, and the excision of discord._

1. **The Rule of Twelve (Dozenians)**
	The Dozenians teach that every person bears twelve resonant chords which must be brought into accord with Mind, Soul, and Body. Their discipline is severe: dozenal prayer cycles, quarter-year fasts, fault ledgers kept with scrupulous honesty until ritually burned. They are most at home in knight-monasteries of the Eternal Dominion and frontier cloisters, admired for iron self-command yet sometimes criticized for paralyzing scruple.

2. **Wardens of the Shining Brazier (Lucentians)**
	The Lucentians hold perpetual watch against buried impurities in people, archives, and places. They staff crypt vigils and ruined sancta, guard sealed reliquaries, and use passages of the Book of Judges as coded watches. Centered on An-Mirda with cells in provincial strongholds, they are tight-lipped but loyal, summoned whenever old condemnations or forbidden caches surface.

3. **Brothers of the Blazing Canticle (Canticlers)**
	The Canticlers practice resonance-purging chant, deploying antiphonal dissonance and exorcistic litanies to unvoice corruption before it roots. Their choirs train under inquisitorial oversight and act only by temporary Flame Writs. Most active in Antidiscordian patriarchates and Dominion courts, they are feared by the obstinate and valued by prelates who prefer cleansing without the sword.

## Harmonism

_A rational-cosmological theology aligning divine order with structural reality._

1. **Circle of Sixfold Similitude (Similitudeans)**
	The Similitudeans map the six elements onto inner ethics, pairing each with a discipline of life and civic duty. Their meetings mix guided resonant hymns with oaths to public service, and their members are common among jurists, engineers, and academy clergy in Lavion, particularly Ertra. They argue that to order the self is to harmonize the community.

3. **Conclave of the Golden Mean (Aureomedians)**
	The Aureomedians treat proportion and beauty as outward signatures of inward harmony. They sponsor sacred architecture, acoustic planning, and geometric iconography, producing sanctuaries famed for calibrated light and sound. Based in Aramoor, Sillaire, and Port Lavandelle, their work makes doctrine audible and visible without lapsing into idolatry.

5. **Brethren Continuant (Continuants)**
	The Continuants insist the Light is process rather than endpoint -- a continuous becoming discerned in recursive form. They pursue aetheric recursion, numerologic prayers, triadic diagrams, and silent proof-vigils. Though marginal to parish life, they are respected by Harmonist scholars for their rigor and by reformers for their humility.

## Concordianism

_A decentralized theology privileging communal concord and post-theocratic humility._

1. **1) Way of Vespertine Contemplation (Vespertinians)**
	The Vespertinians gather at dusk for lay-led hymnody and long, silent contemplation. They are at home in the mercantile cities of the Commonwealth and welcome travelers and apprentices freely, holding that hospitality is a first lesson in concord.

3. **2) Wandering Brethren of Humble Charity (Mendicants)**
	The Mendicants walk circuits of relief under four virtues -- Restraint, Clarity, Charity, and Humility. Their brethren move between markets and hamlets offering bread, bandage, and counsel, and they advise councils only when asked. Strong in Stranu yet visible well beyond it, they seldom speak in Synods and keep to the outskirts.

5. **3) Bridgekeepers of the Faith (Custopontians)**
	The Custopontians treat pilgrimage as cleansing and instruction, and guard it well. They maintain hostels and mediation houses, and escort travelers along coasts and passes under sacred oaths. Concentrated around the Suurian Gate and major inland routes, they are honored by all Patriarchates as neutral custodians of those who journey in the faith. ^92d973

## Redemptionism

_A mystical-pastoral theology of re-attunement; the Light pervades all and calls the detuned back to concord._

1. **Fellowship of the Tuned String (Retuners)**
	The Retuners hold that no soul is mute to Lur's harmony, only out of tune. They lead guided confession and examen circles, pair offenders with those they harmed to craft restitution, and teach reconciliation and amends. They administer small mercy funds and often form traveling choirs who invite all listeners to join their simple yet harmonious canons.

3. **Igniters of the Ichos (Lamplighters)**
	The Lamplighters carry hand lamps into the places people deem lightless -- fetid docks, cramped mines, decrepit prisons. They keep night vigils, retrieve the lost and drunk from gutters, and recite catechisms of mercy and redemption to inmates and gaolers alike. Commonly seen in poorhouses and among the dregs of society, they are known for their rule of gentle interruption.

5. **Menders of the Broken Seal (Restitutionists)**
	The Restitutionists hold that justice must be tempered by mercy so the wider polity may resound in concord. Where the Retuners seek reconciliation as a primarily personal healing, the Restitutionists convene harmony tables with victims, elders, guilds, and magistrates to weigh offences against the common good and compose restitutions that mend public order. They draft binding compacts of service, compensation, and supervised amends; argue against punishments that would seed further dissonance; and press for firm sanctions when necessary to protect the weak and reassure the many.
