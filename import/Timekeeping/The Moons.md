
## Aural
*"Moon, Lur's Mirror"*
The largest of the three moons, and the one defining tidal forces. 
On a clean, repeating 24-day cycle around the planet that forms the basis of the lunar calendar as used by the Remeans.
## Langea
*"Reckoner, Red-Eye"*
A small, red moon far out enough to almost seem a star.
Always visible on the night sky, it moves in an extremely slow 864-day pattern. For its perceived orderly behaviour in tune with a portentous twelve-year rhythm of the sun and the other moons, the Reckoner is given a place of pride in Lurist cosmology.
## Belana
*"Tortoise, Old Ambler"*
A thumbnail-sized greenish moon.
On a wobbly 60-day cycle, influenced by Aural. It forms the basis for the calendar used by the Ukili'ata people (who call it 'Ela'a), as well as Vel Kiriya and other parts of the east.
