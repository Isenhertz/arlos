# Canon decisions from chat sessions

Decisions Jan made in conversation on claude.ai, carried over at the migration to this repo (November 2026). They are canon. Some are already reflected in a lorebook, others are not yet; when one is folded into a lorebook, remove it here in the same branch. Earth-historical shorthand is allowed in this file.

## Setting frame

- Arlos is a secondary world centred on the Circle Sea (Circuleana), a Mediterranean-analogue enclosed sea.
- The Circle Sea sits in the southern hemisphere: south is cold, north is hot (equator-ward).
- The project spans geography, history, religion, technology, language, material science and ecology, developed through an interconnected lorebook corpus used as a writer's bible.

## Cosmology and faith

- The Book of 777 Verses (Lurism's central text) counts 777 in dozenal, with a three-tier structure: the Seven Gross, the Seven Dozen, and the Seven, valued as an oral-recitation mnemonic.
- The Prophet should read as a figure akin to the Buddha, Jesus or Muhammad yet distinct. The Seven Gross recount his life in his own words, open to later reinterpretation, mistranslation and wilful misreading.
- The Prophet's life in the Seven Gross is scattered episodes, not linear biography; commentators argue the sequence for centuries. He was an arbiter-adjacent witness or factor, which grounds the faith's expectation of letters and numeracy. The Theocracy exaggerates his status, walked back after the Sack. His lamp-bearing friend is plausibly Het-Zaphar but never named, so succession has no ready answer.
- The Prophet's flaw is anxiety over an improperly sealed contract (seen more stringently by him than by later Aramoor exegesis), not a full oathbreaking. Arbiters' corruption is a focal point of his journey. His Seven Days on the Stair (a protest that drew An-Mirda's eyes) underlies the highest feast; the solstice alignment may be later artifice over a heathen arbiters' rite.
- The Prophet rails against concrete human practices, never against the elements he venerated, but with enough fervour to generalise into militancy against "those who do things wrong". Post-enlightenment parables are only half explained, needing later commentary and rulings.
- Lurist funerary canon: the pyre is as old as Lurism and the settled western practice. Ground burial is regarded in the west as heathen, evidenced by pre-Lurist dolmens and barrow mounds across the Stranic peninsula, the Varsenne basin and the west coast of Ertra. Crypts persist as communal ash niches. The Eastern Rite buries its dead.
- The foculier's canonical vacancy forms: "the foculier stood cold and unlit" and "no hand held the foculier".

## Technology and materials

- "Magnet", "magnetic" and "magnetism" are acceptable everyday words and concepts in Arlos, since magnets are the natural example of a Tension-active material. Tension remains the preferred term for the Charge itself.
- The technology driver is a thermoacoustic, pneumatic and fulminant progression in place of steam and gunpowder; steam is socially stigmatised and practically absent.
- Shaping (the inward aetheric body-art of the Remean Collegium) is not a simply dystopian overreach but a practice Remeans arrived at naturally from their cultural assumptions. A Remean citizen's experience of it should feel ordinary despite often plainly superhuman capabilities. Its malignment comes from being anathema to Lurism.
- Gliding originates in Lu-Pto (basalt desert thermals, a pre-Remean tribal youth tradition), spreading basin-wide late in the Aetheric Age (after the 1420s, in the later 15th century) as an avant-garde pastime before Lavion's military appropriation after Roburic II's conquest of Lu-Pto. Not a Svenish, Varngardi or purely Lavienese invention.

## Polities and institutions

- Key world actors: the Remean Empire (fallen 0 AR), the Lurist Hierophancy, the Commonwealth of Principalities, the Kingdom of Lavion/Varsenne, the Republic of Varngard, Balboa, Vel Kiriya, the Anish city-states, the Ukili'ata, the Svenish clans, the Dvergr Holds, the Alvai Kin groups, and the Grand Academy of the Broken Isles.
- Vel Kiriya design inspirations: Aksum (highland trading powerhouse) meshed with the Inca, the Mer as a closer Amazon analogue; politics modelled on the Achaemenid Persian Empire; culture and science on the Islamic golden age. These map mainly to the Remean Era and early Age of Enlightenment; later ages are extrapolated.
- Vel Kiriya: tin and gold are its sources of revenue. The road is a central state institution (unlike Remea, which built none), a means of mastering the jungle, maintained by levied labour. The court travels with the seasons, a culture inclined to move with season and circumstance (fitting the Judges and its tolerance). Soil-invocation is heritage of pre-Kharunite animism alongside worship of the mountains and the river. Measure is central to Velkirian thought.
- Vel Kiriya geography: the highlands sit about as far from the Mer-Hal Estuary as the Andes from the Amazon's mouth. The Mer is huge and very long, mostly from wide meanders through the jungle, and must be passable where it leaves the highlands. Highland travel is mostly on foot, with a renowned relay messenger system. Inland Lu-Pto's Sifted Fields are not salt pans; the Lu-Pto coast has salt pans.
- Lavion's Commons (lower house, born of the 1277 peasant rebellion concessions) cannot sponsor legislation directly before the Compact of Virell: bills require a sitting peer or Privy Council sponsor. It has real institutional capacity for organised dissent.

## Terminology

- Svenic: a moling is an elemental sprite of any element (not specifically Air); an adaling is the courtier tier as such (not element-specific).
- Never refer to Remeans as "men". Harmonized Remeans are ambigen (androgynous hermaphrodity), with bodily aesthetics and outsiders' perception reading as female.

## Dark Ages heresies (336-998 AR)

- The Mendicants began as a western "restoration" of the Judges after the fall of the Hierophancy, a return to earlier simplicity: a heresy persisting through the Dark Ages and probably into the Age of Corsairs, resisting full absorption into the mainline church. Mendicants may still claim spiritual descent from the Judges today.
- The validity of oaths sworn to the "wrong" obedience, and opportunistic switching of obediences, is a major political vector of the Dark Ages but not fully heretical; Genovrast suppresses it hard for political reasons, not on Church doctrine. Likewise the Six Relations as warrant for rightful rebellion: the East holds a rescindable mandate (Earth analogue: Mandate of Heaven) that the King of Kings must uphold; no one in the West would countenance it.
- Apocryphal "burned fragments" (from the Sack's archives) and disputes over the order of the Prophet's episodes are recurring doctrinal problems through all ages, most prevalent in the Dark Ages.
- Rural elemental appeasement resurfaces from the Interregnum; ground burial returns where pyres are too costly, the same trajectory of older beliefs resurfacing.

## Remean military (first-principles revision, November 2026)

- Remean weaponry and armour are being reworked from first principles: a maritime bronze-age transhuman thalassocracy prizing aesthetics over brute function until shortly before its end. Shaping stunted material innovation wherever superhuman feats sufficed. The sarissa stays as emblem of Reme shoring up the human phalanx's weaknesses.
- Arcola: the Remeans' own name for their military crossbow, akin to the large early Chinese crossbows. Laminate composite prod on a stock, spanned by superhuman strength, trigger at the rear of the stock for shoulder aiming; massive draw weight, projectiles of a size later armies only used on siege crossbows.
- Arcola final design (empire-era, minor variations): rear-trigger crossbow with nut, sear and trigger lock, a claw riding with the string pulled back via lugs by stirrup and belt-hook (outsider name "belly-bow" from bending over and straightening) or cruder means. Smaller variants hand-spannable by a legionary or even an ordinary citizen. Pistol grip, trigger protected against accidental discharge, pliant-backed butt against recoil. No energy figures are canon (lost weapon); Theocracy records attest its horrific power.
- The sarissa is detachable: the detached half subdivides into a hafted counterweight butt (a cudgel in confined quarters) and a standardised measuring stick. Sarissa heads are not interchangeable.
- Rowers and line infantry are the same citizens: a set of rowers' rows is the smallest unit, the galley the cohort, a fleet the legion at march, scaling up to polyremes as overstrength "prime" units.
- Unit names: spiracion (12 legionaries, from spirax "oar"), belanion (60, glossed "sexaginty", a neologism lining the base-60 unit up with the Roman century), biabelanion (120, double sexaginty), toton (360, glossed cohort; lexicalised from the expected *totion*), estertoton (720, prime cohort). A legion is twelve totons (4,320).
- "One citizen per oar" stays as a rule that confounds contemporaries and later accounts, with a soft gradient of rowers per oar: civilian craft one or two to an oar; military coastal and riverine patrol craft one per oar with about 24-36 oars (two or three spiracionês); belanion two per oar; three per oar from the biabelanion up (four benches per level making a spiracion). A short three-per-oar assault hull is a special assignment.
- Five rowers per oar is unlikely given the spiracion base. The fabled extraordinary polyremes that guarded the uncontested Eastern Gyre used four per oar (a spiracion of three benches on one side, an accepted exception to the either-side pattern) and six per oar (two rows per spiracion, back to the either-side setup).
- The triple-deck "eighteen" is the absolute pinnacle of Remean shipbuilding. A catamaran structure is not ruled out, since the Ukili'ata brought that technique early enough for Remea to have acquired it selectively.
- Raised true brass or bronze is the finer (officer) kit. Armour protects head, throat and torso over limbs as a consequence of Shaping. Iron is the specialist's tool (not specifically anti-elemental). A late functional turn shortly before the Fall is accepted.
- Remea used a proto-hexcraft siege weapon impelling slugs by direct (possibly compounding) modulation of Motility: a railgun without magnets. It did not survive the Fall.
- The Remean sidearm is the alacolis ("great-awl"), a Mycenaean-style rapier or estoc of true brass. Remean vocabulary also has suatra (edged sword, likely a leaf-blade sidesword) and cidestrilis (stiletto). Fencing is a Remean cultural pastime, carried into everyday life by veterans early in the empire's rise.
- Shield darts join the kit for close quarters where the sarissa cannot be brought to bear (for example the failed mountain campaigns against the Moonkin), backing up the alacolis. Grapnels are launched by ship-based impellers.
- Laminate composite bows of various sizes and draw weights are the mainstay of non-legion ranged weaponry. Legion scouts (Shaped, with a recon overlay) use high-draw bows (about 80) as standard. Elite diver units are a must for the Remean forces.
- Stranu (the Iberian-peninsula analogue) is under consideration as a Remean-era tin source, explaining both the empire's presence there and the crusaders' early interest in securing it.
- Working premise on the Remean navy against the Hellenistic and Roman record: every Remean-era naval power could field "tens" with unshaped crews, so the Remean navy's exceptionalism lies in factors other than scaling up rowers (Shaped synchronicity among them).
- Impellers: general Remean term *dinamium*. The smallest class is swivel-mounted, much like a Roman scorpio with nearly no moving parts. Larger classes are the *relemium* ("destructor", the openers) and the *furvex* ("earth-shaker", the siege impeller), cannon-like in velocity and impact, but size and immobility limit them to shipborne and anti-siege use. Open-trough design, exact form left nebulous since the weapon is lost; stage-triggered, the trigger pounded in sequence like a crew hammering a spike or making mochi. Siege work is ship-based (relemium); "anti-siege" means the land-based use of the furvex; inland sieges rely on upscaled *dinamia teniotôs*.
- The furvex is also shipborne: the largest dinamium, likely carried by the twelves and eighteens or by a specialised estertoton hull; its size limits land use to preinstalled sites.
- "Stone-lance" (*lancea lapidea*) is the hostile chroniclers' name for the large dinamia they saw; the Remean name is dinamium. Ranged Arms and Circulean Warfare still describe a single narrow working and need the full family.
- The Arcola goes into Remea.md and Ranged Arms in place of the strength-drawn bow and the "torsion arms". Remea.md keeps its "arrested design" argument, softened to minor variants plus the late functional turn.

## Shaping mechanics and civic life (November 2026)

- The new Shaping lorebook ("Shaping: The Somatic Art of Remea") supersedes earlier Shaping canon; Jan confirms each detail before it is committed.
- Triad in Shaping: the body is the Materiality; the Shaper supplies the Structure; the Resonance is the Shaped person's own (pulse and breath), left open enough that other lost factors may matter. Keying uses a breath technique and singing (specific melodies, augmentative rhythms).
- Shaping cannot supersede the body's workings, only push; like clay or dough, pushing too hard courts disaster. A growing body offers more "clay", which is why Harmonization works best in youth.
- Long War: successive generations of losses likely drove the Collegium to abandon the primacy of feminine aesthetics, producing brute frontliners in place of balanced soldiers, a sloppy caricature of Shaping at its height. These results skirt close to Riv-î-Tep bloodcraft in outcome, which was probably not lost on the Shapers.
- Coercive work cannot be called Shaping (it would undermine the Resonance pillar). What the Sorcerer-Kings did to their subjects is not Shaping.
- Age and enfeeblement pass more gracefully for Remeans but are not beaten. Limb injuries matter far less (bone-shapers address density and brittleness); internal injuries are harder, which is the physiological and psychological basis for protecting head and torso over limbs.
- Harmonization is an integral part of schooling, touching bodily autonomy, self-discovery and growing up. The Lurist view (no individuality, mandatory enforcement) is the harshest possible reading and very likely wrong.
- No daily upkeep: adult Shaping is roughly yearly, like a check-up. Shapers have specialisations and are synonymous with medical practitioners: learned, tested, attested, of high standing. The baths, fed by Ertra's volcanic hot springs, are the natural centre of practice.
- Work and load baselines set to the Shaped body are a natural consequence, good material for contrast scenes with foreigners.
- Structure: singing is part of the Shapers' hands-on practice. Focus pieces are cut from horn, animal bone or whalebone in exacting geometry and worn on pulse points; complex courses need complex pieces (orthodontics and spectacles as analogies). Harmonization likely took all three pulse points, with allowances for healing and other concurrent Shaping. For the most impactful courses the material must come from a living body; brass or pliant serve only as frames or lesser stabilising pieces. Bone is the anchor that holds a completed pattern. Remean skeletons hold the proof of Shaping but are inert, as gryph-bone is inert.
- Focus pieces, once Shaped, are personal and keyed to their wearer. Small cosmetic pieces (a hair comb) may be used day to day by their owner as a minor exception: "no daily upkeep" means none is required. Remea.md and Shaping.md still have the body standing in for all three triad terms and need rewriting to the Shaper-led triad.
- Bonesingers are attested healers in Lu-Pto; at writer level they are a rare, unattested survival of some basic aspect of Shaping in the region furthest from the crusaders.
- Legion Shaping: the civic pass is part of ordinary Harmonization; enlistment Shaping is the legionary's equivalent of a citizen's vocational shaping; campaign overlays are a specialised subset, often created and maintained under field conditions.
- The feminine aesthetic of Harmonization was not strictly necessary but a vestige of matriarchal tribal prestige underlying the original synoecism. Amputation exists, but a mangled limb can be saved by Shapers while one severed by force cannot be mended.

## Conflict resolutions (November 2026)

Decided in the cross-file conflict pass; the dates, names and spellings are already applied, these still need prose.

- Fulminance is in working use well before 1574 (Balboa's Ascensori, Virell's fulminance lines, laboratory cells, state forges). The Fulminant Turn is the post-1574 scaling-up of something that already existed.
- Lavion fields glider troops only after Roburic II's conquest of Lu-Pto (after 1498); Aeronautics' 1480s rediscovery passage moves accordingly.
- Lu-An: Reme holds the iron coasts early; abortive inland excursions against the Zanori and Sunkin recur throughout the empire's life (pattern: Rome's Germanic frontier).
- Grand Academy: founded in the 11th century as a shipyard and officers' training school for corsair-era seamanship and shipbuilding; the 12th-century arcannon later turns it toward engineering. It is the oldest university not theology-first and not directly influenced by a patriarchate.
